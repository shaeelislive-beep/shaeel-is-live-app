shaeel is live app.

A private tool that prepares and schedules Shaeel's own videos to his own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that Shaeel has made and approved to the YouTube channels he owns.
  • Sets each video's title, description, thumbnail and publish time as he chose them.
  • Reads his own channels' video status and statistics so he can see how his videos perform.

The app runs on Shaeel's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
