Privacy policy

shaeel is live app · Last updated 13 November 2026

Who this covers

shaeel is live app is a private tool used only by its owner, Shaeel, to upload and schedule his own videos on the YouTube channels he owns. It is not offered to the public. The only accounts that sign in to it are the owner's own Google accounts.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. Its user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in screen, the app accesses these things for the owner's own channels only:

  • Basic channel information: channel name, handle and identifier.
  • The videos the app itself uploads: their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy setting and publish time.
  • Status and statistics of those videos, such as processing state, view counts and audience retention.

The app does not access other people's accounts, comments, messages or contacts.

How the information is used

  • To upload the owner's videos and publish them at the times he chose.
  • To change or withdraw a scheduled video when he edits or rejects it.
  • To show him how his own videos perform, so he can decide what to make next.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Everything stays on the owner's own computer: the sign-in tokens Google issues, and the status and statistics of his videos. Nothing is sold, shared with or sent to any third party. No advertising or tracking service is used. The app does not use cookies and this website collects nothing from its visitors.

Keeping and deleting

Video statistics are refreshed from YouTube while a video is recent and are kept on the owner's computer until he deletes them. Sign-in tokens are kept until he disconnects a channel. He can remove the app's access at any time on Google's security settings page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; after that the app can no longer reach the channel, and the stored tokens stop working.

Changes

If the app starts handling information differently, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
